# Karigar — Project Scope

Source: `FYP-Pitching-Title-Defense.pptx`

## 1. Problem

- Local artisans have limited online visibility.
- People struggle to discover traditional crafts and the artisans behind them.
- Craft history and cultural value are scattered across sources.
- Museum locations and historical crafts are not discoverable in one place.
- Customers see products without learning about the artisan or craft process.
- Workshop and artisan information is difficult to find.

**Main problem:** no simple platform brings together artisans, their stories,
traditional crafts, museums, historical crafts, collectors and workshops.

## 2. Solution

A single web platform to **Find → Learn → Explore → Connect**:

- Discover artisans and traditional crafts.
- Search and filter by craft and location.
- Find artisans, workshops and museums on a map.
- Read artisan stories and cultural information.
- Follow step-by-step craft-making journeys.
- Explore historical crafts displayed in museums.
- Discover verified collector profiles.
- Buy modern artisan-made products.
- View purchase history and order information.

## 4. Users and permissions

### Customer / general user
Discover crafts and artisans, purchase products, save favorites, view purchase
history, request workshops.

### Artisan
Create artisan profile, share story and craft information, add products, manage
workshop information.

### Museum
Provide museum location, information and historical craft content.

### Collector
Create collector profile, showcase rare craft collections, share collection
stories and knowledge.

### Admin
Manage users, artisans, museums, products and workshops; verify eligible users;
manage platform content and order status; track payments and refunds; review
reported chats.

## 5. Purchase flow

```
Product → Add to Cart → Checkout → Delivery Info → Payment Method → Place Order → Order Status
```

**Account areas:** profile, favorites, cart, orders, purchase history, workshop
requests.

**Payment:** cash on delivery and a sandbox payment gateway (card, wallet or
mobile banking). No full commercial payment gateway. Payment status syncs with
the order, receipts are saved, and admins can track payments/refunds.

## 6. Chat flow

```
Ask a Question → Chat with Artisan → Confirm & Pay → Track Order
```

- Message from any product or profile page.
- Discuss price, materials, delivery or custom orders.
- Share reference photos.
- Notifications and full chat history.
- Report a chat; admins review it.

## 7. Project scope

Responsive web application, authentication, admin management, artisan and craft
discovery, cultural/historical information, product purchasing, museum and
collector information.

## 8. Expected outcome

- Improve online visibility of local artisans.
- Make traditional crafts easier to discover.
- Help users understand craft-making processes.
- Provide accessible museum and historical craft information.
- Connect users with artisan products and workshops.

**Final goal:** a simple platform that helps people discover artisans, crafts,
museums and collectors — and the stories behind them.
